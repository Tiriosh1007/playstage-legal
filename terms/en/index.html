PlayStage is for parents and caregivers arranging play and daily routines from pregnancy through age 6. The account holder must be an adult, or old enough to enter a binding contract where they live. A child must not be asked to create the account.

The app is not directed at children and is not offered as a children’s service. You may add a child profile only for a child you are responsible for.

4. Permitted use

You may use PlayStage to:

  • Read the bundled play ideas, stage guides, communication tips, and theory cards.
  • Keep a family, child profiles, play records, notes, and a weekly plan on that phone.
  • Keep a daily routine list and mark items done.
  • Turn on a reminder that stays on that phone for the signed-in person who is responsible for the item.
  • Write a family activity and attach up to four photos of toys, books, or other materials.
  • Share a family activity to the market on this phone, or download one into your family library. Other phones cannot see that market.

Official weekly suggestions stay the bundled activities. A family activity does not replace that set.

This version has a market only for accounts on the same phone. It is not a website, and other phones cannot see it.
