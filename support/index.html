Support · 支援

PlayStage is a parent-directed guide for play and daily routines, from pregnancy through age 6. It helps a family organise play ideas and a caregiver’s day.

PlayStage 是給家長和照顧者使用的遊戲與每日日程指南，由懷孕到 6 歲。它用來整理遊戲建議和一天的安排。

PlayStage provides informational and organisational features only. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

PlayStage 只提供資料和安排上的功能。它不提供醫學診斷、治療或專業醫療意見。

Data on this phone · 這部電話上的資料

The current app keeps accounts, family, children, play records, routines, family activities, and material photos on the phone. It does not send that information to Firebase. The market is only for accounts on that same phone. Other phones cannot see it.

To remove it, open Profile and choose “Reset local demo data”, then delete the app if you want the copy gone from the phone. The reset also clears this phone’s market listings. It does not reach any other phone, because this version has no shared cloud copy.

現時這個版本把帳戶、家庭、小朋友、玩樂紀錄、日程、家庭活動和材料照片留在電話裡，不會把這些資料傳去 Firebase。市集只供同一部電話上的帳戶使用。其他電話看不到。

要清除，請打開「檔案」，選擇「重設本機示範資料」，再刪除 app。重設也會清除這部電話上的市集項目。這個版本沒有雲端副本，所以不會影響另一部電話。

Later cloud version · 日後的雲端版本

The family-sharing release is planned to store family data in Firebase and to offer account deletion inside the app. That release is not available yet. These pages will be updated when it is, and the date at the bottom will change.

跨電話共用家庭的版本計劃把家庭資料放在 Firebase，並在 app 內提供刪除帳戶。該版本尚未推出。推出時會更新這幾頁，並改底下的日期。
